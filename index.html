Plain-English guide

⚔️ WAR — PRICE & CHART: Is the share price looking strong or weak?

🥀 FAMINE — COMPANY & NEWS: Is current company or economic news helping or hurting it?

👑 CONQUEST — PEOPLE & HYPE: Is attention around the stock calm, fearful, excited or crowded?

☠️ DEATH — DANGER: What could go wrong, and is waiting safer?

🏛️ COUNCIL — FINAL VIEW: Taking everything together, how strong is the evidence?

🔎 EVIDENCE ENGINE: Horseman judges each claim separately — fact, opinion, rumour or prediction — then checks freshness, confirmation, conflicts and possible bias. It does not assume a whole website is trustworthy or untrustworthy.

RSI = how stretched recent price movement is. Momentum = whether price has been moving with strength. Volatility = how violently price moves. Macro = wider economic conditions. Sentiment = the mood around the stock. Crowding = too many people leaning the same way. Liquidity = how easily shares can be bought/sold.

The Council evaluates. You decide. Live data can be delayed, incomplete or wrong; Horseman shows uncertainty rather than hiding it.
